Every V7 line the founder rejected is gone. This deck shows what replaced it, with alternatives where a real choice remains. 1 · Hero — four directions (the build currently runs B)

Every direction keeps the explicit positioning line visible at first paint, before any scroll or hover: LION CREATIVES · CREATIVE & TECHNOLOGY AGENCY

Direction A — concise / iconic

We shoot it.
We build it.

Commercial film and photography — and the web platforms behind them. One team in Maryland.

Why: six words carry the whole company. Risk: reads blunt without strong media beside it — which we have.

Direction B — cinematic + technical in the build

Stories on screen.
Systems behind them.

We direct commercial film and photography — and design and build the digital platforms they live on.

Why: both halves are literal facts, the serif italic carries the cinema half, and the support line says exactly what we sell. Recommended.

Direction C — commercially confident

Your film. Your platform.
One team.

From a 4K commercial to the website that sells it — directed, designed and shipped by Lion Creatives.

Why: speaks to the buyer's project, not our craft. Strongest for conversion; least distinctive as language.

Direction D — editorial / experimental

The camera
and the compiler.

Lion Creatives directs film and photography, then engineers where they live: sites, platforms, systems.

Why: the most ownable image pair. Risk: "compiler" may read too engineering-inside for some buyers.
