What happens next

Three things, in this order.

  1. 01 A person reads it. Enquiries go to the founder, not to a triage inbox and not to a chatbot.
  2. 02 You get a straight answer. A time to talk, one clarifying question, or a no with the reason attached. We would rather decline early than take work we cannot direct properly.
  3. 03 If it fits, you get a shape. Scope, band, and the names of the people who would actually be on it — in writing, before anyone mentions a contract.
